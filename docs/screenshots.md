## Backtest setup / 回测配置

![Actual backtest setup interface](../assets/backtest-workspace.png)

This screenshot shows a setup that has not been run. The displayed 10,000 initial capital is a research parameter, rather than an account balance or profit. It provides no backtest result or evidence of strategy returns. General strategy backtesting belongs to the planned Full scope; Basic includes factor statistics and factor return backtests.

这张图展示尚未运行的回测配置。界面中的 10,000 初始资金是研究参数，不是账户余额或盈利；图片没有回测结果，也不证明策略收益。通用策略回测属于计划中的 Full 范围，Basic 包含因子统计与因子收益回测。

## AI workspace / AI 工作区

![Actual AI interface](../assets/ai-workspace.png)

This screenshot shows the AI entry and configuration interface. It contains no generated answer, forecast result or measured prediction accuracy. AI requires the chosen provider and model configuration, with your own key and provider quotas where applicable.

这张图展示 AI 入口与配置界面，没有生成回答、预测结果或已测得的预测准确率。使用 AI 需要配置所选提供方与模型，按提供方要求自备 Key 和额度。

Any Mainnet label elsewhere in these screenshots has the same limited meaning as the market scanner badge. Historical performance and model estimates do not establish future returns; a research result does not automatically authorize trading or start a bot.

其他截图中的 Mainnet 标签同样只说明当时界面状态。历史表现与模型估计不证明未来收益；研究结果不会自动授权交易或启动机器人。
